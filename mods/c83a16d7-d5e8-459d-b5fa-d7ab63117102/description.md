# Bestiary Displays

Adds **Hologram Projectors**: furniture that shows any creature you unlocked in
the Bestiary as a hologram in the world.

## Projectors

| Projector | Crafted at | Creatures | Scale | Offset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hologram Projector | Anvil | Unlocked in the Bestiary | 0.25x to 2x | Up to 160 px |
| Adamantite Hologram Projector | Mythril Anvil | All | 0.1x to 10x | Up to 2000 px |
| Titanium Hologram Projector | Mythril Anvil | All | 0.1x to 10x | Up to 2000 px |

## Settings

Tap a placed projector to open its menu. The left side shows a live preview
of the creature; the right side has the settings:

- **Select Creature**: pick a creature from a searchable list. Drag the list
  to scroll and tap a creature to show it.
- **Animate**: plays the creature's animation. **Anim Delay** sets how many
  ticks each frame lasts.
- **Hologram FX**: blue tint, flicker, scanlines and glitches.
- **Alternate Render**: cycles through the sprite sheet directly. Use it for
  creatures that don't show up right with the normal drawing.
- **Scale**, **Offset X**, **Offset Y** and **Rotation**: use - and + or tap the
  value to type it.
- **Invisible Block**: hides the projector itself and leaves only the hologram.

The settings are saved with the world and synced in multiplayer.
