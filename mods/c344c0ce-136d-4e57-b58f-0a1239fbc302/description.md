# Terraria Tethers

Ties all players together with a rope, forcing everyone to cooperate.
Ported to Terraria Mobile through Bunny Loader.

## Features

- Players are linked in a chain by a rope that pulls them together when it stretches.
- Teleporting (including magic mirrors) brings every player along.
- Choose what happens on death: all players die, or the player respawns at their respawn point.
- Configurable rope length, tension and elasticity.
- Seven rope visuals: rope, vine, silk, web, chain, chaotix or none.
