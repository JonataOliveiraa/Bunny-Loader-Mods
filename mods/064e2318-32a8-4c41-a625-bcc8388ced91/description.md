# Gnorp's Builder

Meet **Gnorp**, an alien builder who moves into your world on day one. Talk to
him and pick one of his five shops to buy **globes**: use one and the building
appears right where you tapped.

## What you can build

- Lamp posts and small, medium and large houses for every biome (Forest,
  Tundra, Desert, Jungle, Ocean, Mushroom, Hallow), plus "Biome" globes that
  pick the style of the biome you are standing in.
- Castles and tree bases in four sizes.
- Basement modules: rooms, hallways, staircases, corners, prisons, an armory,
  a library and more, that snap together underground.
- Boss arenas (simple and advanced, in three sizes).
- Water, lava, honey and shimmer lakes (shimmer after the Moon Lord).
- Elevators that dig a shaft all the way down to the Underworld, one per biome.
- Pylon houses for every biome.
- Minecart tracks, including one that crosses the whole world.
- Holiday houses (Valentine, heart house) and a few silly ones.

Hallow buildings unlock in Hardmode. If Gnorp dies he drops **Gnorp's Pilot**,
which brings him back (he can also be caught with a bug net).

## On mobile

- The building shows as a translucent preview where it will go.
- By default the building goes with its bottom center on the block you tap,
  so it grows up from the ground and you can see how far it will reach.
- Tap the **Anchor** button (shown while holding a globe) to switch to one of
  the four corners of the original. It is also in the settings.
- Optional "double tap to place" setting, so a stray tap does not waste a globe.
- Works in multiplayer: the server builds and syncs the area.

## Settings

Image preview, dust outline preview, preview opacity, double tap placement and
the default anchor point.

Original mod by **Korlex**, ported by Animatak_. The buildings come from the
original files (StructureHelper is built in, no extra mod needed).
