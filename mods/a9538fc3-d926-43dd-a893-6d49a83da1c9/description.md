# Loot Beams

Dropped items get a **light beam** and a **glow** in their rarity color,
Borderlands style, like Loot Beams for tModLoader (ChemAtDark).

- Rarity colors (modded rarities included), rainbow for Expert items, fiery orange for Master items and
  gold for quest items.
- A flash when the item drops, a pulsing beam, and the beam fades out when
  you pick the item up.
- Coins, hearts and stars get a small beam with their own color, as in the
  original.
- Beam styles (simple, arrow, animated arrow) and glow styles (soft, pixel).
- Optional light around the item.

## Settings

Minimum rarity, minimum value, always highlight quest, Expert and Master
items, beam and glow size and opacity, flash size, brighter (additive) beams,
pulse, light and the default lists.

## `/beams` command (with the item in hand)

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/beams always` | The item always has a beam. |
| `/beams never` | The item never has a beam. |
| `/beams mini` | Toggles the small beam. |
| `/beams color #RRGGBB` | Custom beam color. |
| `/beams reset` | Removes the item from your lists. |
| `/beams list` / `/beams clear` | Shows or clears your lists. |
| `/beams on` / `/beams off` | Turns beams on or off. |

Available in 12 languages.
