# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Port of NPC Unlock Announcer 1.1.0 to Bunny Loader: the six banner styles,
  the announcement sounds, banner duration, announcement frequency and the
  subtitles for every vanilla Town NPC, saved per world.
- The banner scales with the interface size and fits the screen width on
  phones.
- New Preview button in the settings to see the banner.
- Text in 12 languages.
