# Flat Seed

A world so flat you can run without fear of tripping into a hole! The surface
becomes perfectly flat, and everything else is generated as usual: caves,
biomes, ores, the dungeon, the temple, the floating islands and the underworld.

## How to use

It is a **secret seed**. When creating a new world, type one of these codes in
the seed field:

| Language | Codes |
|---|---|
| English | `flat`, `flat world`, `superflat` |
| Português | `plano`, `mundo plano`, `superplano` |
| Español | `plano`, `mundo plano` |
| Français | `plat`, `monde plat` |
| Deutsch | `flach`, `flache Welt` |
| Italiano | `piatto`, `mondo piatto` |
| Polski | `płaski`, `płaski świat` |
| Русский | `плоский`, `плоский мир` |
| 中文 | `平坦`, `平坦世界`, `平面世界` |
| 日本語 | `平ら`, `平らな世界` |
| 한국어 | `평평`, `평평한 세계` |
| Tiếng Việt | `phẳng`, `thế giới phẳng` |
| ไทย | `แบน`, `โลกแบน` |

It also shows up in the game's secret seed list, so it can be combined with
other secret seeds. Only new worlds made with it are flat; your other worlds
are not touched.

## World generation

During generation you will see the new step **Flattening the world...**: it
levels the surface, smooths the ramps down to the beaches and keeps a layer of
dirt on top. Hills, tunnels and surface lakes are left out. The floating
islands are kept whole, sand included.

With the **Mole People** secret seed (no surface), the world is not flattened.

## Options

In the main menu: **Settings > Mod Configuration > Flat Seed**.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Surface height | Where the flat ground sits, as a percentage of the world's height (20% by default). |
| Fill chasms | Also fills the holes and chasms that open on the surface. |
| Beach ramps | A smooth slope from the flat ground down to the oceans. |

## Notes

With content mods, the screen may look frozen for a moment during world
generation: it is still generating, just wait.
