# Player Ping UI

Shows your ping in multiplayer and opens a panel with the ping of every
connected player.

## How to use

- In multiplayer, the ping indicator sits **next to your health bar**, at its
  left end and centered on it: the icon changes with the connection (full, mid,
  low, empty) and the number below it, in milliseconds, goes from green to red.
- Turn off **Next to the health bar** in Mod Configuration to place it
  anywhere: then you **drag** it with your finger.
- **Tap** the indicator to open the settings: the icon style and how many
  seconds between ping checks.
- The **player board** button, at the top of the screen, opens the server
  panel: each player with their head, name, ping and the icon they chose.
- Nothing shows in single player.

The ping is really measured: the device sends a packet to the server and times
how long it takes to come back. The icon style you pick shows for everyone in
the server panel.

## Styles

Purple, Basic, Nintendo DSi, Gold, Basic Light, Gold Fancy and Minecraft. You
can also change it in Mod Configuration, along with the option to hide the
indicator.
