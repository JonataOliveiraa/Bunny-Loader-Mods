Teto Vanity Sets adds a lot of variations of **Kasane Teto** as vanity sets, plus
wings, backpacks, a hairstyle, two pets and an NPC who sells it all.

> [!TIP]
> Craft a ![](content/Content/Items/Placeable/Furniture/TetoPlush.png) **Teto Plush** (15 [i:225] Silk on a [i:36] Work Bench) and keep it in
> your inventory: **Kasane Teto** moves in and sells every set. The pages of her
> shop are listed on the **Shop** tab.

## Vanity sets

need only a body and legs). Every piece is a vanity item, and a few sets turn

## Hairstyle and pets

- ![](content/Content/Hairs/TetoHairstyle.png) **Teto's Hairstyle** shows up in character creation and at the Stylist.
- ![](content/Content/Pets/Pearto/PeartoItem.png) **Pearto** hops after you and flies when you get far away.
- ![](content/Content/Pets/Dragon/DragonItem.png) **Sukina Souzai Happyou Dragon** flies with you.

## Kasane Teto

The ![](content/Content/NPCs/TetoNPC/TetoNPC_Head.png) **Kasane Teto** NPC moves in when someone has a Teto Plush
in the inventory. She sells every set, and 1 in 25 times she drops a baguette.
Use the **Change Shop** button to flip through her pages.
