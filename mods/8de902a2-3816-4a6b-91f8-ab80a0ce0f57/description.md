# Teleport Keeper

Create and manage **Teleport Points** around the world and travel between them
whenever you want.

## Touch buttons

In the game's control editor (Settings > Controls, a custom layout, Edit), add
the mod's actions at the end of the list:

- **Create Teleport**: opens the creation panel. Tap the name to type it (or
  keep the default name), pick the icon and the name color and, if you want,
  check **Delete the teleport when used**.
- **Teleport List**: every teleport in the world, with search. On each one:
  **Go**, **Edit** (name, icon, color and delete when used) and **Delete** (tap
  twice to confirm). Tap the **star** to favorite it: favorites come first.
  Above the list, filter by **All**, by favorites or by a biome's icon.

## Icons

The icon is picked by the biome you are in (Forest, Snow, Desert, Jungle,
Corruption, Crimson, Hallow, Mushroom, Ocean, Underground, Cavern, Underworld,
Dungeon, Temple, Shimmer, Sky and Graveyard) and can be changed when creating
or in the list, which also has **Home**, **Arena**, **Space** and the default
crystal.

## Death Teleport

Each player has their own, created where they died. It is gone when its owner
uses it or leaves the world, and it is not saved. Any player can delete it in
the list.

## On the map

Open the **map** and tap a teleport's icon to travel to it.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/tp [name]` | Saves a Teleport Point where you are. |
| `/deltp <number>` | Deletes a Teleport Point (its position in the list). |
| `/clearall` | Deletes every saved Teleport Point. |

Teleports are saved in the world and, in multiplayer, are the same for every
player. In-game texts in English, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, French, German
and Russian.
