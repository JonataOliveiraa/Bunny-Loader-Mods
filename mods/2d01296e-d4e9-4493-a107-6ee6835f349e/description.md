Show off your best gear! A ![](content/Content/Items/DisplayBaseItem.png)
**Display Base** holds any item and shows it with glitter, an outline and a soft
light, like Item Display Base for tModLoader (Cleyera).

> [!TIP]
> Make one from 6 [i:3066] Smooth Marble Blocks or 6 [i:3086] Granite Blocks at
> a [i:2172] Heavy Work Bench. The full guide is in the **Crafting** tab.

## Two styles

| Base | Material |
|---|---|
| ![](content/Content/Items/DisplayBaseItem.png) Display Base (marble) | 6 [i:3066] Smooth Marble Blocks |
| ![](content/Content/Items/DisplayBaseItemGranite.png) Display Base (granite) | 6 [i:3086] Granite Blocks |

## How to use

- **Tap the base** while holding an item to put it on display. Weapons, tools,
  summon staffs, potions: anything goes.
- Tap with an **empty hand** (or a favorited item) to take it back.
- Tapping with another item **swaps** it with the one on display.
- With a **tool**, a quick tap puts it on display; **hold** the finger to mine.
- Hitting a base drops its item first; an empty base breaks normally.

## Effects

The item on display glitters, gets a shimmering outline and lights up the area.
Each effect can be turned off in the settings, and the light strength goes from
0 to 2.

> [!NOTE]
> The item stays on the base when the world is saved, and is synced in
> multiplayer.

Available in 12 languages.
