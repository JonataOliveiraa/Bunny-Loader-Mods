## Getting the materials

- [i:3066] **Smooth Marble Block**: made from [i:3081] Marble Blocks, found in
  the Marble biome.
- [i:3086] **Granite Block**: mined in the Granite biome.

::: spoiler Why two bases?
They work the same way: the granite base is just a darker look for your
display room.
:::
