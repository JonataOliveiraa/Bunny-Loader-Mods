# Extended Zoom Out

Three toggles:

- **Farther Zoom**: on = 125% zoom-out limit, off = normal.
- **Smooth Zoom**: pinch and slider zoom ease toward the target.
- **Pinch can't open the map**: pinching out past the limit no longer opens the map, but the map still pinch-zooms once you open it with the map button.

- **Smooth Map Zoom**: pinch zoom on the fullscreen map eases toward the target like the player zoom does.
