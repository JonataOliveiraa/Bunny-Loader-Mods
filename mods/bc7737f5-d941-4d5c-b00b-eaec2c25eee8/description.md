# Magic Storage Adaptation

## Overview

Magic Storage Adaptation brings the Magic Storage experience to Terraria on mobile. Build a network of Storage Units around a Storage Heart, then manage everything you own from a single touch-friendly screen: search it, sort it, take items out, put items in, and craft straight from what the network holds.

It uses Magic Storage's textures and is built around its concept, rewritten from scratch to run on mobile devices.

## Features

- **One screen for everything:** every item in a connected network, browsable from a Storage Heart or Storage Access.
- **Search, filter and sort:** tap category icons to stack filters, search by name or #category, and sort your storage the way you like.
- **Recipe browser:** every recipe shown in green when craftable and red when something is missing, with the ingredients and stations it needs.
- **Recipe slot:** hold any item for a moment, move it with your finger and drop it on the faded hammer slot at the top to jump to the Crafting tab with every recipe that makes or uses it.
- **Hold and drag:** hold a stack to pick it up (it bobs above your finger) and drop it on the storage, the inventory or the stations list to move it.
- **Craft from storage:** craft one, a batch or the maximum using materials held in the network, with the vanilla crafting sound.
- **Installable crafting stations:** install stations (and Graveyard items) into the network so their requirements are always available.
- **Seven capacity upgrades:** grow a Storage Unit from 40 to 640 slots without losing its contents. The unit's light shows green when empty, yellow when partly filled and red when full.
- **Remote access:** link a Storage Remote to a Storage Heart and open your network from anywhere.
- **Quick Stack support:** the game's own Quick Stack to Nearby Chests button also deposits into storage networks within range.
- **One stack button:** tap to quick stack your inventory into storage; hold it to switch to Similar stack, which also stores items of the same category.
- **Made for touch:** tap to take or store, batch buttons for crafting, and an on-screen keyboard for search.

## Crafting Recipes

Unless otherwise stated, basic blocks are crafted at a **Work Bench**.

### Basic Blocks

- **Storage Component:** 10 Wood, 2 Iron Bar or Lead Bar
- **Storage Unit:** 1 Storage Component, 1 Chest, 10 Silver Bar or Tungsten Bar
- **Storage Heart:** 1 Storage Component, 2 Diamond or Shadow Diamond, 3 Emerald
- **Storage Connector (16):** 16 Wood, 1 Iron Bar or Lead Bar
- **Storage Access:** 1 Storage Component, 1 Diamond or Shadow Diamond, 3 Topaz
- **Storage Crafting Interface:** 1 Storage Component, 1 Diamond or Shadow Diamond, 3 Sapphire
- **Storage Remote:** 1 Storage Component, 5 Sapphire, 5 Soul of Light, 5 Soul of Night
- **Combined Crafting Station:** 1 Storage Component, 1 Work Bench, 1 Furnace, 1 Iron Anvil, 1 Sawmill, 1 Loom, 1 Cooking Pot, 1 Keg, 1 Bookcase, 1 Tinker's Workshop, 1 Alchemy Table, 1 Crystal Ball

### Materials

- **Shadow Diamond** — crafted at a Demon Altar: 1 Diamond, 5 Shadow Scale or Tissue Sample
- **Radiant Jewel** — crafted at an Ancient Manipulator: 1 Luminite Bar, 1 Diamond

### Storage Upgrades

Craft these at an **Iron Anvil or Lead Anvil**.

- **Demonite Upgrade:** 10 Demonite Bar, 1 Amethyst
- **Crimtane Upgrade:** 10 Crimtane Bar, 1 Amethyst
- **Hellstone Upgrade:** 10 Hellstone Bar, 1 Topaz
- **Hallowed Upgrade:** 10 Hallowed Bar, 1 Soul of Fright, 1 Soul of Might, 1 Soul of Sight, 1 Sapphire
- **Blue Chlorophyte Upgrade:** 5 Shroomite Bar, 5 Spectre Bar, 2 Beetle Husk, 1 Emerald
- **Luminite Upgrade:** 10 Luminite Bar, 5 Solar Fragment, 5 Vortex Fragment, 5 Nebula Fragment, 5 Stardust Fragment, 1 Ruby
- **Terra Upgrade:** 10 Radiant Jewel, 1 Diamond or Shadow Diamond

### Storage Capacity Progression

| Tier | Capacity |
|---|---:|
| Basic | 40 slots |
| Demonite or Crimtane | 80 slots |
| Hellstone | 120 slots |
| Hallowed | 160 slots |
| Blue Chlorophyte | 240 slots |
| Luminite | 320 slots |
| Terra | 640 slots |
